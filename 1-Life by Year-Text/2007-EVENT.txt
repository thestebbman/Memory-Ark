Sullivan and associates- So I got let go from Sullivan Associates because I filed too many complaints against employees who were not doing their jobs or abusing people with disabilities. When I first started working first Sullivan and Associates the house they gave me was a complete disaster. This was my first experience as a manager of anything. It took me like two months to get that house in working order and after I was done it ran so smoothly that I made everyone else in the company look bad and because of that I started to make a lot of enemies.

First off I created a standard form for medication to bring to the doctors so that there would be no more confusion. I don’t understand why the state doesn't have a standard form for every company to use, instead they all pick and choose. The manager was allowed to create whatever type of form they wanted when bringing clients to the doctors and i thought that was stupid. At first doctors gave me crap because I forced them to use my paperwork, but eventually they preferred doing things my way, agreeing that it prevented confusion and made it easy for everyone for medical professionals and house staff to follow.
Unlike other houses I started making staff interact with the clients and bring them places. We started going to the gym every single day and people around the company complained, not the clients but other staff. I used to take the clients to the gym by myself and then we'd go to big Y or Stop and Shop to buy whatever they would like for dinner, then we would grill it. 
Before I started, all the guys ate was pasta and hotdogs every night, I thought that was the most disgusting thing I had ever heard in my entire life. After work with me for a couple of months these guys were in the best shape of their lives, we went to the casino a couple of times, we went to the mall all the time, we went hiking all the time, and 1 of the guys had leg problems it was supposed to wear leg braces but stopped wearing them because he loved walking without them,  it got to the point where doctors said he didn't need them. I also started filing a lot of complaints about the abuse and neglect I was witnessing in other homes and by staff members that worked in the house that I managed. 

I was causing so many problems by fixing things that my program manager figured he’d try to stress me out and started pressuring me to work at other locations and purposely gave me kids that they considered troubled, but didn’t give me enough information to really understand what was going on and left me in situations where I’m positive they were hoping I’d fail.

2007 Flossie G Patrie Died 12-2-2007  Born Flossie Gero 5-3-1914 So many happy memories , Francis Gero sister

Aunt Flossie’s death marked the end of a generation for me. I’m not sure how other people remember this, but I feel like so much was lost with that last connection to the past. I had found it heart breaking that she had outlasted siblings she raised, she raised Francis and Norman after her mother died, also Pearl and Leo had also passed in recent years. 
I think the thing stuck with me the most is how once again my uncle Chet arrives too late to say goodbye to someone while they’re coherent. By the time Chet showed up aunt Flossie couldn’t speak and was having trouble breathing. 

Up until this point I had been visiting once a week for at least 15 minutes. I had spent more time there in the past, but her vision was going, she wasn’t able to get around as well, so she spent a lot of time in bed and I really didn’t know what else to do. Norman was such a crabby ass when I came and he was there all the time at this point, so I wasn't able to have friendly chats like I would have preferred.

I only mention Norman being crabby because on one of my visits aunt Flossie had been talking about living on the farm in upper state New York and was telling about how they used to chop wood all the time, then Norman chimed in and gruppily let us know that he doesn’t have time to do stuff like that.
