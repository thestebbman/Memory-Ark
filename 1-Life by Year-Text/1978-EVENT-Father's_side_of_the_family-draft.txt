I thought it might be easier to separate my memories of each side of my family, since they were different and there weren't any large or memorable events where families co-mingled.

===

My first memories of my father's side of the family are pretty vague. I don’t know why I would think I would have far more impressionable memories.

My fathers side was larger than my mothers and there were a lot more kids, so this was a totally different experience growing up. It allowed me to act more childish than I normally could.

The Stebbins all used to live within a couple miles of each other. My grandma lived in the same house on Moss Rd for 50 plus years, she lived in that house for 30 years after grandpa Stebbins passed, which must've been rough and I never appreciated how empty a home could feel until I got older.

===

My dad's oldest brother, uncle Dave lived a couple miles away on Middlebrook Dr.

We cut down trees, helped get ready to install their above ground pool. I remember trying to cut a tree with an axe and it was impossible for me. Maybe it was a splitting maul or maybe I was weak or the blade was dull, but I recall I was bummed to learn I didn't have what I takes, lol

===

My first memories of uncle Mike and his kids are of them living on Starling Rd, we used to go over all the time and have sleep overs. I'm pretty sure they had an above ground pool here. They had a long backyard, so we were able to play whiffle ball and soccer all the time.

and then moved next door to Grandma Stebbins on Moss Rd. Aunt Anne owned the house prior. It seemed like living close to family was what everyone did back then. Aunt Anne had one of those pianos that could also play itself. I can’t recall how many family parties we went to while she lives there, but I also recall Suzie living there for years.

Now families aren't as close and corporations ruin everything.

===

Aunt Mary is younger than my dad by a few years, she didn't have kids until we were older, she lived with grandma Stebbins, though eventually moved to Huntington, MA near where they hold the Westfield Wild River race, though I never stopped to visit. I never felt that close to aunt Mary.

===

My dads younger sibling, my uncle Mark lived on Plum St. I used to think the woods setting wall-paper they had in their living room looked awesome and I thought it was the coolest that their basement opened up to the woods, but I never got to explore. At one point I recall them trying to fill in some of the back yard. There was a slope that led down to a ravine, but I'm not sure if it was from the old stream course or the natural shape of the area.

We used to go over my grandparents house on Moss Road all the time, not just for family events, we went over randomly to visit all the time and would drive by and stop to say hi if someone was outside.

===

We held family picnics at a couple different locations over the years, Look Memorial Park in Northampton and at the Hampden memorial park in Hampden, they used to call it the rec or recreation area.

I'm not sure if we started at Look and then changed to Hampden, but we went to Look a few times and I went back with Laura for a visit as an adult in 2014 or 2015.

Look park was awesome, there was a river that flowed right next to the picnic area, there was also a dam or break to slow water down so we could walk across and toss food at the fish. They had a trolley or train like forest park in Springfield, though I don't recall going on it. There's also a zoo there and paddle boats you can rent.

Going to the Hampden Rec was different, maybe it was because they had the softball fields right there and the playground. When we were younger we played kickball, this was one of the few games where we could get almost the whole family involved, whether watching or playing. We used to all gather to watch and support who was playing at the time.



1980's

My dad and uncle Mark were coaches for our soccer team for a few years, uncle Mike helped, I feel like we were really lucky to have a dad that was so active in our lives, though my getting sick and appearing to slack caused friction in our relationship, I can’t complain to much, we do the best with what we have, plus I got to play on a team with my brother and cousin Joey, that was awesome.

We went to countless birthday parties. all my cousins and I got dragged along to the adult events. I can hear people telling us to settle down as I write this

Dad worked at Hoppe tool in Chicopee and I'm pretty sure his friend Rick Morin worked here too. We only had one car for years, so we got to drop him off and pick him up on numerous occasions.
