1995
January
Driving grandma Francis’s Chevrolet Chevette was an experience in itself. That car was a basic as they make them, it didn’t even have power steering, had a stiff suspension, it had the crappiest radio, but it got us from point A to point B. lol
Grandma Francis was the best. She gave us the “vette” when she bought her Chevrolet Celebrity and we drove it for years. I wish I could recall when we got rid of it. We had the van around this time too. 

Learning to drive in a vehicle that’s top heavy like our old van is scary. There were more than a few occasions I caught myself going to fast and the road was tighter than I thought I was going to be, like heading to the Forni’s on Mapleshade Ave and being sure we were going to tumble, while everyone else sat calmly and paid little to no attention at all. I wonder if my anxiety was what made these moments feel like they were more than they were, since I normally find driving exciting, but I recall being afraid back then and there was no sense of excitement at all, not even after everything was fine. I never acknowledged that as an over reaction being. 

1995-1-25
01-25-1995
Looking back, I'm surprised that no one took this more seriously at events like this. Granted these situations were all just jokes to me, but Tammy had died a few months before, no one knew what happened and no one appeared to be looking into anything. It’s the same 30 years later.
I really wish I could remember what I was accused of doing or saying. I only remember feeling like the people involved were acting stupid and trying to twist what I was saying. I probably told them they were full of shit. lol

Basement Door.






I can't remember exactly why I was in the principal's office for this incident, but needless to say I didn't respect the police at this time, my friend Tammy had recently died and there were still no answers, cops were acting weird around the school. 
After Tammy was found I had a few female friends make goofy offers to go fool around in the subbasement, but they would always tease me with things I didn’t think were going to happen. Needless to say I never snuck down for any mysterious rendezvous but I would lie and say I did, which got these girls to admit that "something came up” lol . I thought this was just a joke until one time I had been invited to go down, but I didn't, though I did walk by that door on my way to my next class. In between classes one time I happened to walk by one of the sub-basement doors and this weird police officer pops out and accuses me of giggling the handle trying to act like I was attempting to go down there. lol I’m not sure if he had the door crack and had been peeking or heard some noise and thought he’s was going to get lucky and get me to admit to some nonsense, but I was 10-15 away and walking towards the stairs, which were straight and to the left and the door he exited was to the right, facing me.
I was totally caught off guard and focused on him accusing me of touching the handle, not on being invited down there. When looking back I’m curious if he was the one telling those girls to lure me down in the dark, where officers would be waiting to scare me. lol I’m lucky the idea of fooling around with girls in school didn’t appeal to me or I could’ve gotten into all kinds of trouble. Lol

Summer


It was somewhere around this time that someone had gotten hit by a train on the railroad tracks over by Five Mile Pond and for whatever reason Mindy and I went for a walk over there, and it was pretty gross. You could still see chunks of flesh everywhere and I saw what looked like an elbow or shoulder joint along the tracks. I find this stuff both gross and fascinating at the same time. I probably heard someone had been hit, but didn’t actually expect to see anything. 


August
 Right around this time I bought a 1987 Mercury Grand Marquis. It looked like an old person's car, it had tons of space and I loved it. It had a 5.0 engine, not that I really understand what that means even now, lol but it was fast and handled very well. 



It was around this time I also got a job as a prep cook at Olive on the corner of Boston Road and Parker Street. This is the first job where I ever had any real responsibility, but it sucked working alone.

 I thought it was a lot of fun making the different sauces and soups. I learned how to make Alfredo, it was simple prepping the meat sauces, cooking the chicken tenders and meatballs. Though I did tap the inside of the machine we cooked the meatballs in once, and oh man did that hurt like crazy. lol
I got to have all the free soup, salad and breadsticks I wanted as well. Though I mainly ate breadsticks and banana peppers. Lol
One time someone left a clear plastic sheet on the floor and I slipped, fell flat on my back and cracked my head on the floor. This was when I had come in early to help the main crew, since I normally worked nights. It’s been 30 years and I still remember how bad that hurt. Lol This is what happens when you work with other people, you need to be extra aware of all the things that can and will eventually go wrong. I got to finish the night shift alone like I always did, so this day was pretty rough. 



September

I think it was during this soccer season, either way, we had a game next to the cemetery and I remember my cousin Kenny found some monster bouquet of flowers and brought it over to his mom in the middle of the game and she was mortified. lol 
My Aunt Sandy hollered at him to return it, he probably found it in a pile, I doubt he stole it off of somebody's grave. lol It was probably from compost piles, I know cemeteries collect plants that people leave on their visits.

November




I got suspended for singing in the song "I touched myself by the Divinyls" because it was on the radio in our shop and some Haggard old teacher's aide felt the need to report me for singing it. lol 
Looking back I think it's total bullshit, and they should never even have allowed the radio to be on in the first place, especially if they're going to complain about the language being used "on the radio". 
I sang this more than a few times at karaoke years later. lol Though it is not one of my favorite songs, I just think it's funny and stupid. lol



 I got to spend 30 days downtown at a building that looked like a castle, I got to do all my homework all at once. Then I was able to play chess and basketball for the rest of the day. If educators were trying to teach me a lesson, sending me to this place didn’t do the job. Not only did I think the building was beautiful, I had a blast while I was here and left like I got along great with everyone there. 

I shared a picture below of the old school even though it’s part of the casino in 2026. I didn’t get to explore this building. Looking back I should have asked, they might’ve said yes.
