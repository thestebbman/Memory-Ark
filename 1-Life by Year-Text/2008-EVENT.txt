February
The Rex Lounge in Chicopee: Fist Fight Night 




The 7th (date on Banks 2021 paperwork) 
It started as a normal night of Karaoke. I was there with my dad, Mary W, Darlene, B, Reno, Tony Cross, Erica Larner and a bunch of other regulars including Dino and Kathy.
I swear it was not my fault, I was minding my own business. Either way, we were all sitting at a table by the front door, Darlene was sitting across the table from me, with her back to the wall, Reno was sitting to my right and we were just talking, goofing around and waiting for our turns to sing karaoke. 
Then this guy that looked just like Magnum Pi, he had the 80s hair and mustache, he was tall, muscular, and I guess he felt like it was his lucky night. He came over to our table, leaned in close to Darlene and started getting flirty. 
I guess it started with him whispering stuff in her ear, but at some point his mustache brushed her neck, so she asked him to stop. lol
I remember someone said something to me, but I didn't witness this incident, so I didn't care, lol  I wasn't going to get upset over something I didn’t see, so I just ignored it. I assumed people were just feeling the alcohol. 
A few minutes go by and this guy's back over next to Darlene and the same thing happened again. 
The next few moments were like slow motion. 
I remember hearing Reno ask this guy to nicely to step away, he said something like; she's asked you not to do that, and that's when this Magnum Pi looking guy says to Reno- "you want a punch in the mouth" and then he just hauls off and punches Reno in the mouth. lol 
Looking back now, I don't think that guy punched Reno that hard in the mouth, but it was the heat of the moment, so I just responded. 

Before Reno even had time to touch his lip, I burst from my chair, I shoved Reno out of the way, which incidentally threw him to the floor, which I didn't even realize. lol poor Reno

I just started punching this guy in the face, his head started bouncing off the wall, I hit him like 15 times, then my father yanked me away and this guy felt at a floor, blood pouring from his nose and mouth. 

I saw Tony and Erica were trying to help him up, then in the heat of the moment, I don't know what the fuck I was thinking, I yanked myself away from my father, then I soccer kicked this guy in the face, before he even had a clue what was going on. 

It was horrifying, I've never seen a body just drop limp like before. In my defense, this guy was a lot bigger than I was at the time and he just started swinging, so I didn't know what to do, I just reacted and it was ugly. 

When the cops arrived, they started congratulating me, they said this guy has been going to local bars and picking fights with all kinds of people. The people in the ambulance were laughing because I broke this guy's nose and he had a 3 inch gash across his forehead. They said I was about time this guy got his ass kicked. 

But honestly I felt like such a piece of crap. 

I remember going to the bathroom after they took him away and looking in the mirror, my hands were shaking and I couldn't believe what happened, I felt so bad because that guy had to be taken off in an ambulance. 
I felt strange that I like such a piece of shit while everybody was congratulating me, they thought it was such a great thing. I tried to pump myself up and go along, but it felt so fake, I didn't really feel good about beating that guy unconscious.

I think the worst part for me was that my right hand and my right foot both hurt for over a month after that. Every time I went to the gym I was reminded of that fight. I was bothered because I felt like it shouldn't have happened or there should have been another way, I still felt like I was in the right, so what are you going to do lol

Court after 
Even though that Magnum Pi guy was in the wrong, Reno and I still had to deal with this nonsense in Chicopee district court,


Reno ended up hiring an attorney, this lawyer was a complete buffoon, he didn't seem to have a clue what was going on, he even mentioned that this could take months and that I might want to hire an attorney.

I ended up arguing with the prosecutor. I thought it was a bunch of bullshit that Reno and I should have to deal with this shit at all. Reno was a victim and yeah sure, maybe I could appear guilty for defending someone else, if that’s a thing, but I only hit that guy because he sexually harassed someone, then punched someone in the face after someone politely asked him to stop.

I was furious that I was expected to explain myself over and over. I demanded to know where all the statements were from the witnesses that night and I had a list of people that were ready to testify, I thought it was fucking bullshit we had to deal with this at all.

I guess the prosecutor agreed with my way of thinking, they had us sign a paper that claimed we “didn't recall the details of that night and weren't going to pursue charges against the other parties”, we agreed it was all just a big misunderstanding. lol wtf


Then this buffoon of a lawyer has the nerve to tell me that I should become a lawyer or an investigator. 

After these charges were dismissed this Magnum Pi looking guy still wanted to fight me, I guess he was a boxer, felt like I had cheated and he still had something to prove. lol 
All because he hit my friend and then I hit him, cause and effect. lol

Some people never grow up, this guy was 10-15 years older than I was, he shouldn't have been acting like an angry/spoiled child, just because things didn't go his way and here he was looking to get knocked out again. lol Like I would trust this dickhead to stand toe to toe in a fair fight. Lol
Needless to say the fight never happened, I’m not sure if he changed his mind or if he was just blowing off steam.

I think it was December I started doing cross stitching at work. I started getting really bored just sitting there alone and it was giving me anxiety. This is also around the time that my elbows started to really burn as well. 
My forearms felt like they were on fire all the time, it was so bad I tried ice packs to cool them down and the doctor didn’t believe me.
So I found hobbies that keep my mind focused. I tried building models, but I wanted to paint them and at times answering the phone would get in the way of gluing parts, so I new painting would be a nightmare and wouldn't allow me to switch focus to work instantly without making a mess.

I also used their printer paper and went crazy making all the different origami animals I could find online. At the time I think I had 40. some were variations of different folds, though it doesn't matter because I needed instructions, I couldn't just remember and fold all these complicated designs from memory. That's not how my memory seems to work these days, though I can fold some nice paper air planes and I did while there too. since the second floor had a balcony that overlooked the first floor entry way, I did try throwing paper planes from time to time. I also got to learn how to use the waffle maker, so I would try to fatten myself up when I got really bored. 

I also bought my dad an RC helicopter for Christmas and crashed it in the lobby, since the ceiling was so high, I thought I was safe, but I wasn't feeling right and my reaction time was slow, so I crashed and broke it, before ever gave it to him. lol which gave me the opportunity to learn how to fix it before Christmas. 
That thing didn't last long, my brother tried it, flew it up, I guess it flipped and flew into the ground, so that was the end of that. Ultimately a bad idea, but with funny memories to go with it. 

I'm not sure what got me to settle on cross-stitching, but I think butterflies are beautiful and the work was a slight challenge, it required me to focus and it gave me something to look forward to as these patterns emerged. Once I knew where I was, I could pick which area I wanted to finish first.
