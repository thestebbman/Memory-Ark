In my search to fulfill my assignment I came across an article in Infotrac titled Why 

Americans are devout and diverse but not divided  by Stephen Bates, the article was 

originally posted in The Christian Science Monitor back in 2009. It is an opinion 

about  religious tolerance in America which touched on  Americans changing  faiths,

religious intermarriage, to how most houses of worship have become easy going and 

how by defining deity down ,Americans keep the faith.

I found that I agreed with this whole article for several reasons.  Every where I look be it

television or news articles, examples of religious intermarriage can be found, declines in

religious density have lead to drops in service worship followed by billboards and signs 

lining front lawns begging for donations to keep the faith alive in the hopes of preventing

church closings.  Even the television shows I watch have characters from all Religions 

interacting without conflict promoting tolerance of other peoples beliefs. 


After reading this article several questions came to mind. First was what place does 

Religion have in my life? This question leads me in two different directs. The first is 

of a more personal experience and starts thirty two years ago when I was forced as a small 

child to attend Catholic church every Sunday, I even went to Catholic school for seven years starting in first grade 

and I hated every minute of it. I can not explain what thoughts were running through my 

Head to bring about such feeling, never once could I honestly say that I believed there 

was a God. Every moment I spent in church mimicking the actions of those around me 

whether it was knelling and praying, accepting the Eucharist or shaking the hands of 

those around me was solely to shut my parents up. 

Even now as an adult I still feel the same. Although I would like to believe that there is an 

almighty being watching over me and the ones I love, I find it hard to swallow. I see to 

much pain and suffering of innocent peoples , to many prayers go unanswered, to many 

people doing  terrible things in that name of their God. So I find myself living by that old 

adage” Do unto others as you would have them do unto you.” 


The second direction my first question leads me in is my love of history. Everywhere I 

look into the past there is religion. I find it totally fascinating and I can never learn 

enough about the beliefs or the actions brought about by different religions all throughout 

the history of every culture. Though I do not let their beliefs or actions effect me on a

personal level.

The second question I found myself asking was could I marry someone of a different 

religion?  This answer would most likely depend on my perspective wife’s dedication to

her said religion. I do not want to be with anyone who will try to convert me to their

religion just as I would not try to convert anyone to my beliefs or dissuade them from 

their own beliefs. Just 

as important, I do not want to hear about God on a daily basis. My ability to tolerate other 

peoples religious beliefs coincides with my ability to walk away from situations I feel I do

not wish to be a part of.  Living under the same roof as someone with strong religious 

ties would most likely leave me feeling that they are fanatics and I would move on 

without the need to have any contact with that person again. 



The third question that came to mind is how do other peoples religious beliefs affect my 

daily life? Given my line of work I interact with a multitude of different people on a daily 

basis and for the most part I am not aware of their religious beliefs. Aside from the 

obvious signs such as Jewish males wearing Yakima’s and Muslim women wearing their 

Hijab. As a whole I find the people around me to be kind courteous human beings simply

going about their lives without the need to interfere with others.

The final question that popped into my head was how do I feel about the drop in the

number of people attending services due to the decline in religious density (which I am 

sure also has to do with the current economic situation)? The answer to that is simple, I

hope people stop going all together. I do not believe that need to attend services at all, 

they can pray anywhere they want and being catholic I am sure my perspective is tainted 

by the fact that all the priests focus on is people donating more money to the church and 

in recent years the scandals that have come to light concerning all the pedophiles that 

litter the catholic church as a whole.


At the end of the day, I do not feel I will remember anything specific about Stephen Bates 

Or his article. The idea that there is religious tolerance in America has been around long before 

him and it was be around long after. I found more interest in the results of the polls he 

quoted than the context of the article itself.
