Dian Fossey was an American Zoologist born January 16,1932 in San Francisco, 

California that completed an extended study of gorilla groups over a period of 18 years in 

the mountain forests of Rwanda. Her work had initially been encouraged by famous 

Paleontologist Louis Leakey. Fossey was considered one of the worlds foremost 

Primatologists while she was alive and was a part of Leakey’s Angels, a group of three 

Prominent primate researchers sent to study great apes in their natural environments by 

archaeologist Louis Leakey.


Education:

Fossey received a bachelors degree in occupational therapy from San Jose State 

College in 1954 after transferring from a pre-veterinary course in biology at the 

university of California, from there she went on to become the director of the 

occupational therapy department at Korsair Children’s Hospital in Louisville, Kentucky.  

In 1976 Fossey received her PhD from Darwin College, University of Cambridge for her 

thesis “ The Behavior of the Mountain Gorilla.” She also lectured at Cornell University in 

Ithaca, New York as a Professor between 1981 and 1983.


Work:

By 1966 Fossey had gained the support of Dr. Leakey, and through him, funds to carry 

out long-term research on the mountain gorillas. She began her field study at Kabara, in 

the Democratic Republic of Congo (then Zaire), but by 1967, political upheaval forced 

her to move to Rwanda. on  September 24, 1967 Fossey founded the Karisoke Research 

Center in the Virunga Mountains in the Ruhengeri province, Rwanda.  Fossey became an 

international celebrity after her picture appeared on the cover of National Geographic Magazine in 1970.  She supported Active Conservation( anti-poaching patrols and natural 

habitat preservation) and opposed zoos and tourism because gorillas are susceptible to 

diseases carried by humans like the flu and tourism interferes with natural wild behavior.

Fossey created the Digit Fund ( now know as the Dian Fossey Gorilla fund international 

in the USA and the Gorilla Organization in the UK)  after her favorite Gorilla Digit was 

killed by poachers during the day on New Years Eve 1977.  She is also responsible for the 

revision of a European Community project that converted parkland into pyrethrum farms. 

Thanks to her efforts, the park boundary was lowered from the 3000-meter line to the 

2500-meter line.

Fossey wrote Gorillas in the Mist which was published in 1983, The book had covered 

Fossey’s scientific career in great detail and left out details of her personal life.

The book went on to become a movie in 1988, it was produced by Universal Studios and 

Warner brother studios starring Sigourney Weaver and it remains the best-selling book on 

gorillas of all time. 


Death:

On December 26, 1985 Fossey was found brutally murdered in the bedroom of her cabin, 

her head had been split by the same machete she confiscated years before and decided to 

keep as a wall decoration.
