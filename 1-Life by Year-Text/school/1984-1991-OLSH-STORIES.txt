
Our Lady of Sacred Heart

1984-1991



1st grade 



The beginning of every year was exciting. We got new backpacks, new pants and shirts, new sneakers, and new writing journals. We also got to make book covers with paper bags on all our hardcover school books, so that we wouldn’t damage them, and we would all draw different things on them/customize them. 



First started mountain crayons on the radiators, Got my GoBots taken away. 



Halloween using paper bags for masks was the greatest. I would do this at home after I learned about this. lol 



We used to sing all the time and make arts and crafts for all the holidays. 



We used to get teachers gifts at the end of each year. I always liked giving little decorative bells, since they used them to get our attention. They must have gotten hundreds over the years.



2nd grade



I think this is the first time ever hearing that song, John Jacob Jingleheimer Schmidt. lol I still sing that and try to get it stuck in people’s heads. 



At some point, they painted four square blocks in the parking lot, and I was obsessed with playing. I think a lot of us were. It sucked, waiting in line for your turn. lol



It’s somewhere around this time too that I remember that I started to pull my sleeves up. And I still do that to this day on all my sweatshirts and long-sleeved shirts. One of the teachers had commented about me being cold in the winter and I said oh I’m fine. But as an adult, I can’t handle the cold at all and I’m a sissy. I don’t know if that’s appropriate to say anymore. lol But fuck it, the last time I ran outside in the cold as an adult with a tank top on to get the mail, I almost passed out because I back and chest cramped so bad. I had to lay down in bed for like 45 minutes to warm up before my muscles released and I no longer felt like I was going to bend over backwards. 



It’s funny how rude and like totally aloof some kids are, like myself. I remember standing in line after lunch and waiting to go to recess and I just coughed on the back of one kid’s neck and they were disgusted And I apologized, but I didn’t pay any attention to what I was doing, and I just coughed. I don’t know why I didn’t think before I acted on many occasions. 



I can’t remember what grade it was in, but I lost a tooth while at lunch in the cafeteria and I remember being totally distraught that I wasn’t gonna get my money from the tooth fairy. Lol I wanted to stay and look for it so bad but the teachers didn’t care. They didn’t feel my pain. lol 







I’m not sure if those second or third grade, but I remember having to read out loud and deciding to read in a funny voice, then the other students started to make a fun of me, but I kept reading that way anyway. I remember feeling super anxious at the time and reading with a funny voice made me less anxious about reading out loud. Remember the teacher telling the kids to make fun of me and that it was being creative, But they still make fun of me. lol I didn’t care I thought the way that they read was boring and I thought that school was boring. lol 





I think it was second grade that I used to get in trouble for poking holes in the seats on the bus. I used to remove the little push pins on the bottom of the chairs that helped them slide across the tiled or stone floors. It was annoying for anyone who had to sit in these chairs after I removed one of these pins. lol 



I got in trouble for drawing all over my desk, and I remember the teacher and principal making a big deal about it and then I had to clean it. I think it took me like two minutes once I sprayed it and then the pencil washed right off. At times I felt like people enjoyed to make a big deal over nothing.



I can’t give an exact number, but I know that I threw a lot of things out of the windows while I went to school. So many paper airplanes, crayons, pencils and the covers to pencil sharpeners made their way out onto the sidewalk below and were picked and brought to the office by people walking by. lol 





3rd grade



I’m not sure what year, but we got to dissect frogs, and since I was already used to using frogs as bait and eating frog legs in New York, this experience wasn’t all that strange, but I do remember it smelling awful. I thought it was cool because I got to partner up with Keith. I can’t remember if we had our own frogs or if we had to share; I also don’t remember finding the experience all that enlightening. It was more like, so what? Why are we doing this? lol I never need to cut open a dead animal and look at it again. 



I heard that other kids had dissected pigs and kittens and puppies at another school, so I guess I’m glad that I only had to dissect a frog. I wasn’t good at it, and that was the only time I think that we ever got to use the science lab. I had always wanted to go back in there and mess around with stuff again, but we never got to. 





They had these school tag sales where the older kids would bring in all kinds of stuff, and then we bought tickets and used those to buy whatever was being sold. I can’t remember if it was the eighth graders that were doing this or what I remember always wanting to buy toys and never having enough tickets for what I wanted. lol There was always some Transformers or some G.I. Joe that I didn’t have, and I was obsessed with toys, so my heart sank when I had to let them go. Lol 



I think this is a year that my brother got in trouble for wandering around with me, even though he was in class at the time. lol I can’t remember what I was doing. Maybe I’d gotten sent to the office for getting in trouble, but I had taken that opportunity to wander around the school and explore. I’d gone into the gymnasium, and then if you went through the doors in the back, there was a stairwell that led up to some little bleacher area above the gym. I also tried to climb up this metal ladder in the wall at the top of the stairwell to see if I could get on the roof, but that didn’t work; there was a lock. After I had told some older kids about this, one of them also happened to be wearing a cast. I’m pretty sure it was Joe Demars. I can’t remember the other kid’s name, but I think I could figure out who he was if I had enough time. Anyway, someone had seen the other kids exploring the upper area of the gym and assumed it was my brother and me, even though my brother had never followed me around school and explored. His teacher was obviously blind since he was sitting in her class the entire time. I can’t remember exactly what had happened, but he got in trouble too. lol 



I would know for sure if this was the year that this happened if I could figure out what year my brother broke his wrist while we were playing at the playground at Five Mile Pond. I used to love going there before they tore down the awesome wooden play-set that they had. One time, when my brother was going across the monkey bars, he landed wrong and broke his wrist, and then we had to go to the hospital in Ludlow so he could get his cast.



I remember driving around with my dad afterwards and crying. lol I told my dad that my brother’s hand would’ve fallen off if he didn’t have skin. lol 

I used to say the funniest thing as a kid. Obviously, that’s wrong. The ligaments and tendons would’ve held his hand on if he didn’t have skin. lol 





The year that Mario Brothers three came out, someone had bought it and left it in a plastic bag on our bus within like the first week of it coming out. One morning when my brother and I were on our way to school, and we were all rushing onto the bus, another kid had rushed ahead of me and happened to find it on the seat, then grabbed the bag and threw it at me without looking to see what was in it. I opened it, saw what it was and snuck the game into my brother‘s backpack before anyone saw what it was or where it went. I think Joe Frame threw it at me. Lol 

I can’t even describe how excited I was when that happened and to get home and play it. 



4th grade



The class got to go on a train ride for the outing this year and for some reason I didn’t get my permission slip signed. So I was left to sit alone in class by myself all day, and I remember staring at the window and wandering around the desks. It was such a weird experience, sitting by myself with no adults around and not having a clue what to do. 



The next day, the class was all excited because Danny Sweeney had broken his finger while messing around with the window on the train. 

We all joked around for weeks about him sitting on his finger and other stupid stuff. lol I was such an ass. I did use the call him Danny weenie.. But he had a great sense of humor. He used to call me “Stebbies wipes’. lol  ”. 



I used to love getting toilet paper wet and throwing it at the ceiling in the bathroom. lol There must’ve been 100 clumps of toilet paper stuck to the ceiling and light covers because of me. 





It was sometime around here that I found that key ring that was a penis with wings. I’ve gotten it from that old German couple that lived behind us at one of their tag sales. The kids in class thought it was hilarious, and one of us threw it onto the roof. Like a lot of other items, it stayed there. 



They had these craft bazaars in the gym every holiday season and my parents would take us. Grandpa Pete had a table there where he would sell his little wooden toys. There was a lot of religious-themed knitting items, and I think it was more for adults than it was for kids, But I loved going anyway.



5th grade



I was such a little shit. I can’t remember what grade this was in. But our bus used to pick up Danny Sweeney and his brothers and one time I tied a shoelace across the legs of the seats, and when they came running on the bus, Danny tripped, and they all piled on him. The shoelace snapped when he stumbled, and he didn’t notice that he actually tripped over anything so he just got up and went and sat down like nothing happened. Lol 



6th grade



I can’t remember what year it was, but I was an altar boy for like a year, maybe. I thought it sucked. lol I thought church was so boring and I thought that standing there as an altar boy was so weird. I wanted to make my parents proud, but I didn’t want to do this stuff. I thought it was gonna be an honor and I thought it was gonna be educational, but I didn’t learn anything.



It was just, stand here while I do this. They never explained why they were doing anything or what the importance of doing it was.



I can’t remember what year they had the dance, but I remember dancing with one girl, Teresa, and she kept telling me to stop moving because I kept trying to move in circles. lol She was in a grade above me, and I don’t think she was very happy to have to dance with me.  I was such a dork. Lol





It was sometime around six or seventh grade that I hung out of the second-floor bathroom window and jumped into the parking lot. My cousin Kenny and his friends are at recess and the teachers went around. lol I don’t know what I was doing or why I thought that was a great idea, but it worked out and I made it. 





7th grade



Sister Mary Fatima, I remember, she used to always point things with her middle finger and we would laugh like it was the funniest thing in the world And she would get annoyed with us and constantly told us to knock it off, but I don’t know if she was aware of what we were laughing at all the time.



I just remember her being very serious and not having a sense of humor, so she and I were not best friends.
