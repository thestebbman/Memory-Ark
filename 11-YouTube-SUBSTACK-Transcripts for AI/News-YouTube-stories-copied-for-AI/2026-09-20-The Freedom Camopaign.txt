Macklemore and Ms. Rachel have each independently pledged $1 million in support of Palestine.
Their commitments are powerful examples of what solidarity can look like when it becomes action.
In that same spirit, Doctors Against Genocide is launching the Freedom Campaign, our own commitment to raise $5 million to support Gaza’s children and sustain its devastated healthcare system.

The campaign will help:
Provide lifesaving medical care
Support hospitals and healthcare workers
Care for wounded children, mothers, and families
Sustain and rebuild a healthcare system devastated by genocide and siege
Doctors Against Genocide works closely with healthcare leaders on the ground in Gaza to identify the most urgent needs. Every project is carefully vetted to ensure that the need is real, the plan is feasible, and the support can reach the people and institutions it is intended to serve.

Our members and supporters are the backbone of Doctors Against Genocide. Together, we have achieved significant milestones through advocacy, education, humanitarian support, and collective action. The Freedom Campaign is the next step in what we can accomplish together.

On September 20, we will hear directly from healthcare leaders, clinicians, engineers, emergency responders, and humanitarian advocates. Each speaker will address a vital area of Gaza’s healthcare needs and explain what is required to sustain, restore, and rebuild the system.
