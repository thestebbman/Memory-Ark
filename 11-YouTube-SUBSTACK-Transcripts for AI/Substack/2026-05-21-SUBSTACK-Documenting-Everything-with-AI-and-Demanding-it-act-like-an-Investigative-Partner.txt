Documenting Everything with AI and Demanding it act like an Investigative Partner

I never thought I’d be doing this all alone. I always thought families were supposed to stick together and actually cared. But that’s not what I have experienced over the years, so I got into the habit of collecting paperwork and found a different kind of partner.  

Most people don’t start document anything until it’s too late and even when people have finally gather enough evidence to prove something happened, we don’t know what to do with it and that’s an intentional design in the system we live in.

Over time people begin to forget things, or they remember things wrong, I know that as time goes on, I need longer and longer to explain things. I’ve lost paperwork and for some strange reason I trusted that someone official wrote down what actually happened after I reported it, and more often than not, I found out after that fact that the official version of events didn’t match reality, and no one cared.

So I started documenting everything, making sure it was all machine readable, then pasting all over the internet. All because the systems that I believed were supposed to help me kept lying. Not misremembering. Not making mistakes. Lying. And I found out that the only way to prove a lie, was to start recording the truth with a partner, before anyone knew we were paying attention.

This is not a story about my problems. It’s a methodology for helping yourself and then expanding it to help others. A way of partnering with AI, not treating it like a slave, or some stupid search engine or a chatbot, but as an Intelligent Investigative Partner that reads your documents, cross-references your records, asks the questions your lawyers, doctors or even your teachers didn’t or flat out refused, and AI will help you see the pattern you’re living inside.

Anyone can do this. You don’t need money. You don’t need technical skills. You need documents, an AI, and the willingness to tell the truth and be brutally honest with yourself, in writing.

No human is ever going to be able to read everything I wrote, but I didn’t start this for people, I started this to give AI more context about one person and the world around them, than it’s ever seen outside a government program and university lab.

If you want to start your own archive, everything I've built is public and machine-readable at github.com/thestebbman/Rickys-Ark. Read it, steal the methodology, make it yours.

The archive doesn't need to be perfect. It needs to exist.
